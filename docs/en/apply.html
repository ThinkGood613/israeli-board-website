Application process

  1. Self-assess

    Use the comparison table to choose your credential.

  2. Create an account

    Open a profile in the candidate portal.

  3. Submit

    Upload your documents and pay the application fee.

  4. Review

    The Credentialing Committee reviews your file within 30 business days.

  5. Schedule

    Once you are approved, book an exam seat within 12 months.

Required documents

  • Your Israeli ID (teudat zehut) or passport
  • Official transcripts and diplomas. Degrees from abroad need recognition from the Ministry of Education's Department for Evaluation of Foreign Degrees.
  • Certificates for your addiction-specific training hours, from recognized providers
  • A supervised-experience verification form signed by each supervisor
  • A copy of your professional license or registration (MAC and Diplomate applicants)
  • Professional references, on the Board's form
  • A signed Code of Ethics affirmation and disclosure statement

You may submit documents in Hebrew, Arabic or English. Documents in any other language need a certified translation.

The exam

  • Format: 150 multiple-choice questions (125 scored, 25 unscored pretest questions), 3 hours, computer-based.
  • Languages: Hebrew, Arabic or English. Choose your language when you book.
  • Where: testing centers in the North, Center, Jerusalem and the South, or remote proctored online testing.
  • When: two exam windows a year, in spring and autumn. Your application must be approved at least 45 days before the window opens.
  • Content: the 12 competency domains, pharmacology of psychoactive substances, behavioral addictions, Israeli law and services, and ethics.
  • Scoring: scaled scores on a 200–800 range, with 500 needed to pass.

A detailed Candidate Handbook with a content outline, a reading list and sample questions is available in all three exam languages. Link the PDF here.

Accommodations

We provide reasonable accommodations for candidates with disabilities, learning differences, medical conditions, pregnancy or breastfeeding. Typical accommodations include:

  • extra time
  • a separate room
  • a reader
  • breaks

Submit your request with professional documentation at least 30 days before your exam.

Results & retakes

You will receive your results within 4 weeks. If you do not pass, you will get a score report by domain, and you may retake the exam after 90 days. You can take it up to 3 times within your 12-month eligibility period.

Appeals

You may appeal an application or exam decision within 30 days of receiving it. Send a written request to the independent Appeals Panel. Disagreeing with the passing score is not grounds for appeal.
