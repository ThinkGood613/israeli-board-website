Apply & Exam

Application process

  1. Check eligibility

    Compare your education and supervised hours with the CASAP requirements.

  2. Submit

    Send your application and documents to ICBAP and pay the application fee.

  3. Review

    ICBAP reviews your file and confirms your eligibility for the exam.

  4. Take the exam

    Register for and pass the IC&RC ADC exam.

  5. Get certified

    Receive your CASAP certificate and join the public registry.

Required documents

  • Your Israeli ID (teudat zehut) or passport
  • Your diploma or degree certificates, with transcripts
  • Certificates showing your 300 hours of addiction-specific education, including 6 hours of ethics
  • Signed verification of your supervised work experience from each supervisor
  • A signed ICBAP Code of Ethics

Confirm the document list and the accepted languages for documents.

Transferring an IC&RC credential

If you hold an active IC&RC ADC credential from another member board and are moving to Israel, you can apply for CASAP through reciprocity. Your current board sends ICBAP verification of your credential, and you do not need to retake the exam.

If you hold the CASAP and are moving abroad, contact the IC&RC member board in your new location to start the transfer. ICBAP will confirm your credential to them.

The IC&RC ADC exam

The exam is developed by IC&RC and used by member boards worldwide, so it is the same exam ADC candidates take everywhere.

  • Format: computer-based, multiple-choice.
  • Content: the four ADC performance domains: screening, assessment and engagement; treatment planning, collaboration and referral; counseling; and professional and ethical responsibilities.
  • Eligibility: ICBAP approves you to sit the exam once your application is accepted.
  • Preparation: IC&RC publishes a candidate guide and study materials for the ADC exam on its website.

Confirm with IC&RC: number of questions, time allowed, languages available, and testing locations or remote testing for candidates in Israel.

Accommodations

Candidates with a disability or medical condition can ask for reasonable accommodations, such as extra time or a separate room. Send your request with supporting documentation when you apply.

Appeals

You may appeal a decision on your application in writing within 30 days of receiving it. Confirm ICBAP's appeal period and process.
