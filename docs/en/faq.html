Frequently Asked Questions

Can't find your answer? Contact us.

Certification

Is Board certification required by law to work in addiction treatment?

No. Board certification is a voluntary professional credential. Some employers and funders may prefer or require it, but it does not replace statutory licensing (for example, as a social worker, psychologist or physician).

Which credential should I apply for?

That depends mainly on your degree and your supervised hours. Have a post-secondary certificate? Apply for CAC I. A bachelor's degree, for CAC II. A master's degree and a professional license, for MAC. Use the comparison table to check.

Can I count hours I accumulated before registering with the Board?

Yes. Hours from the last 10 years count if a qualified supervisor can verify them.

I trained abroad. Can I apply?

Yes. Your degree must be recognized in Israel. If you hold a credential from a recognized international board (for example IC&RC, NAADAC or ISAM), you may qualify for a shortened review.

Do you certify peer recovery specialists?

A Peer Recovery credential is in development. Update when available.

The exam

What languages is the exam offered in?

Hebrew, Arabic and English.

How should I prepare?

Study the Candidate Handbook, which includes a content outline, a reading list and sample questions. Board-approved providers also offer exam preparation courses.

What happens if I fail?

You will receive a score report by domain. You may retake the exam after 90 days, up to 3 times within your 12-month eligibility period.

Renewal

When do I need to renew?

Every two years, by the expiry date on your certificate. The portal sends reminders 90, 60 and 30 days before.

I was called up for extended reserve duty. What happens to my renewal?

You get an automatic 6-month extension. Upload your service confirmation in the portal.

For the public

How do I check whether a therapist is certified?

Search the public registry by name or certificate number.

Can the Board recommend a therapist or treatment center?

No. The Board does not refer clients. The registry only confirms credential status. To find treatment services, contact the Ministry of Health's addiction services or your health fund (kupat holim).
