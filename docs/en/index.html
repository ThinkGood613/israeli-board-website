Internationally recognized certification for addiction professionals in Israel

The Israel Certification Board of Addiction Professionals (ICBAP) awards the CASAP credential to counselors who meet the international standards of the IC&RC. CASAP is reciprocal with IC&RC member boards around the world.

The credential

CASAP: the IC&RC Alcohol & Drug Counselor credential for Israel

CASAP is ICBAP's name for the IC&RC Alcohol & Drug Counselor (ADC) credential. Holders have completed addiction-specific education and supervised experience, passed the IC&RC ADC exam, and committed to a code of ethics.

How it works

Your path to the CASAP

  1. Check eligibility

    Compare your education and supervised hours with the requirements.

  2. Apply to ICBAP

    Send your transcripts, training certificates and supervision records, and sign the Code of Ethics.

  3. Pass the exam

    Once approved, take the IC&RC ADC exam.

  4. Get listed

    Receive your CASAP certificate and appear in the public registry.

  5. Stay current

    Complete continuing education and renew on schedule.

Israel Certification Board of Addiction Professionals

The Israel Certification Board of Addiction Professionals (ICBAP) is a member board of the International Certification & Reciprocity Consortium (IC&RC). It awards the CASAP credential, Israel's equivalent of the IC&RC Alcohol & Drug Counselor (ADC) credential.
