Credentials

A clear career ladder, from first role to senior clinician

Our credentials follow international practice (IC&RC, NAADAC, ISAM) and are adapted to Israeli law, training routes and communities.

CAC I

Certified Addiction Counselor I

Entry-level credential for counselors with a post-secondary qualification and supervised experience.

CAC II

Certified Addiction Counselor II

For counselors with a bachelor's degree in a behavioral or health field.

MAC

Master Addiction Clinician

Advanced credential for licensed clinicians with a master's degree.

Certified Clinical Supervisor

Specialty endorsements: certified CAC II, MAC and CCS holders can add a specialty in Gambling Disorder, Compulsive Sexual Behavior, Gaming & Digital Behaviors, or Co-occurring Disorders. Physicians can be recognized as an Addiction Medicine Diplomate.

Why board certification matters

Certification tells clients, families, employers and funders that a professional has met an independent, verified standard of knowledge, supervised practice and ethics.

How it works

Your path to certification

  1. Check eligibility

    Compare your education and supervised hours with the credential requirements.

  2. Apply online

    Upload transcripts, supervision logs and references, and sign the Code of Ethics.

  3. Pass the exam

    A computer-based exam held twice a year, in Hebrew, Arabic or English.

  4. Get listed

    Receive your certificate and appear in the public registry.

  5. Stay current

    Complete continuing education and renew every two years.
