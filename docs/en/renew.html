Renew & Continuing Education

Certification is a commitment to lifelong learning. Credentials are renewed every two years.

What counts as CE

  • Courses, workshops, conferences and webinars from Board-approved providers. These are accepted automatically.
  • Academic courses at a recognized institution: 1 academic semester hour = 15 CE hours.
  • Teaching or presenting a new course: up to 10 hours per cycle.
  • Publishing in a peer-reviewed journal: up to 10 hours per cycle.
  • Courses from other providers. These need individual approval; submit the agenda and your certificate.

Self-study and online courses may make up no more than half of your total hours.

How to renew

  1. Log your CE hours in the portal throughout the cycle.
  2. Submit your renewal up to 60 days before your expiry date.
  3. Attest that you comply with the Code of Ethics and disclose any relevant legal or disciplinary matters.
  4. Confirm your professional license is current (MAC and Diplomate holders only).
  5. Pay the renewal fee.

CE audit

Each cycle, 10% of renewals are randomly selected for audit. If you are selected, upload your certificates of attendance within 30 days. Keep your CE records for at least 4 years. Misrepresenting CE hours is an ethics violation.

Inactive & retired status

  • Inactive: for parental leave, illness, military reserve duty, study or relocation abroad. Allowed for up to 2 years. You may not practice under the credential during this time.
  • Retired: for professionals who have left clinical practice. You may keep the title with the suffix "(Ret.)".

Late renewal & reinstatement

For 90 days after your expiry date, you can still renew by paying a late fee. After that your credential lapses and the registry shows it as "Expired".

Within 2 years of expiry, you can reinstate by completing all the missing CE hours. After 2 years you must reapply and retake the exam.

Military reserve (miluim) service: certificants called up for extended reserve duty get an automatic 6-month extension. Contact us with your service confirmation.

Approved CE providers

Universities, colleges, hospitals, treatment centers and professional associations can apply to become Board-approved CE providers. Their courses are then accepted without individual review.

Provider requirements

  • Qualified instructors and evidence-based content
  • Clear learning objectives
  • Attendance tracking and participant evaluation
  • A complaints procedure

Approval lasts 3 years. Approved providers may use the "Board-Approved CE Provider" mark.
