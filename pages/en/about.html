Who we are

ICBAP certifies addiction professionals in Israel to international standards. We award the CASAP credential, which is reciprocal with the IC&RC Alcohol & Drug Counselor (ADC) credential.

Our IC&RC membership

The International Certification & Reciprocity Consortium (IC&RC) is a non-profit organization that protects the public by setting internationally recognized credentials and exams for prevention, substance use disorder and recovery professionals. Its members are more than 75 certification boards in US states, US territories and countries around the world.

ICBAP is listed as Israel's member board in the IC&RC Member Board Directory, offering the ADC credential at the reciprocal level. In practice this means:

  • CASAP candidates meet IC&RC's minimum standards and pass the IC&RC ADC exam.
  • CASAP holders can transfer their credential to other IC&RC member boards if they relocate.
  • ADC holders from other member boards can transfer their credential to ICBAP.

Mission

To protect the public by certifying addiction professionals who have shown the knowledge, supervised clinical competence and ethical commitment needed to provide safe, effective care.

Our values

Public protection

Our first duty is to clients, families and communities.

International standards

We hold Israeli professionals to the same standards as IC&RC boards worldwide.

Cultural competence

Israel's communities are diverse. We expect counselors to serve all of them with respect.

Fairness & transparency

We publish clear criteria, apply them consistently and offer a right of appeal.

Certification and licensure

CASAP is a voluntary professional credential. It works alongside, and never replaces, licensing and recognition by Israeli authorities, including the Ministry of Health, the Ministry of Welfare and Social Affairs, and the professional registrars for physicians, psychologists and social workers.
