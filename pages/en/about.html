About the Board

An independent, non-profit body that sets and upholds professional standards for those who treat addiction and compulsive behavior disorders in Israel.

Mission & vision

Mission: to protect the public by certifying professionals who have shown the knowledge, supervised clinical competence and ethical commitment needed to provide safe, effective, evidence-based care for people affected by addiction and compulsive behavior disorders.

Vision: that everyone in Israel affected by addiction, in every community and in every language, can reach a qualified, accountable professional.

Our values

Public protection

Our first duty is to clients, families and communities, not to the profession.

Evidence & competence

Our standards follow current research, ICD-11/DSM-5-TR classifications and best international practice.

Cultural humility

Israel's society is diverse: Jewish, Arab, Haredi, Ethiopian-Israeli, Russian-speaking and new-immigrant communities. Our standards and exams reflect that.

Fairness & transparency

We publish clear criteria, apply them consistently and offer a right of appeal.

Scope of practice

The Board certifies professionals working across the full continuum of care for:

  • Substance use disorders: alcohol, cannabis, opioids, stimulants, sedatives, nicotine and new psychoactive substances
  • Gambling disorder
  • Gaming disorder and problematic digital behaviors
  • Compulsive sexual behavior disorder
  • Co-occurring mental health and addictive disorders

This includes prevention, assessment, outpatient and residential treatment, harm reduction, recovery support, family work and clinical supervision.

Certification and licensure. Board certification is a voluntary professional credential. It works alongside, and never replaces, statutory licensing and recognition by Israeli authorities, including the Ministry of Health, the Ministry of Welfare and Social Affairs, and the professional registrars for physicians, psychologists and social workers.

Governance

The Board is a registered non-profit association (amuta) Reg. no. 58-000000-0, governed by bylaws and overseen by an elected board of directors. Its standing committees are:

  • Credentialing Committee: reviews applications and recommends certification decisions.
  • Examination Committee: develops and validates the exam with subject-matter experts and psychometric support.
  • Ethics & Professional Conduct Committee: reviews complaints and recommends sanctions.
  • Continuing Education Committee: approves CE providers and courses.
  • Appeals Panel: independent members who hear appeals against Board decisions.

The board of directors includes a public member with no professional ties to the field and a representative with lived experience of recovery.

International alignment

Our standards were designed with reference to leading international bodies, including the International Certification & Reciprocity Consortium (IC&RC), NAADAC / NCC AP, the International Society of Addiction Medicine (ISAM), the International Gambling Counselor Certification Board (IGCCB) and the Colombo Plan Global Centre for Credentialing and Certification (ICAP). We aim to pursue reciprocity agreements so that Israeli credentials are recognized abroad.

Do not claim formal affiliation with these bodies until agreements are signed.
