CASAP Certification

ICBAP awards one credential, the CASAP. It is the Israeli equivalent of the IC&RC Alcohol & Drug Counselor (ADC) credential and is reciprocal with IC&RC member boards around the world.

About the CASAP

CASAP · IC&RC ADC

CASAP [full credential name, to confirm]

For counselors who provide screening, assessment, treatment planning and counseling to people with alcohol and other drug use disorders. CASAP holders have met IC&RC's international standards for education, supervised experience and ethics, and have passed the IC&RC ADC exam.

IC&RC reciprocity

ICBAP is listed as Israel's member board in the IC&RC Member Board Directory. IC&RC is an international consortium of more than 75 certification boards that share common standards and exams.

Because CASAP is reciprocal at the ADC level, a CASAP holder who moves abroad can transfer the credential to another IC&RC member board without retaking the exam. Professionals who hold an IC&RC ADC from another member board can likewise transfer it to ICBAP when they move to Israel.

Reciprocity transfers the credential. The receiving board may also require local training, such as Israeli law and services, before issuing its own credential.

Requirements

These are IC&RC's minimum standards for the ADC credential. Member boards may add local requirements.

  • Education: a high school diploma (bagrut) or equivalent, at minimum.
  • Addiction-specific training: 300 hours of education in the ADC performance domains, including 6 hours of professional counselor ethics and responsibilities.
  • Supervised experience: 2,000 to 6,000 hours of supervised work in the ADC domains, depending on your degree. See the table below.
  • Exam: pass the IC&RC ADC exam.
  • Ethics: sign the ICBAP Code of Ethics.

ADC performance domains

The training, supervised experience and exam are all built around IC&RC's four ADC performance domains:

1. Screening, assessment & engagement

Building rapport, screening for substance use and co-occurring conditions, and completing a full assessment.

2. Treatment planning, collaboration & referral

Writing individualized treatment plans, coordinating care and referring to other services.

3. Counseling

Individual, group and family counseling using evidence-based approaches.

4. Professional & ethical responsibilities

Ethics, confidentiality, documentation, cultural competence and supervision.

The IC&RC ADC exam

CASAP candidates take the same IC&RC ADC exam used by member boards worldwide. It is a computer-based, multiple-choice exam. See Apply & Exam for format, scheduling and preparation.
