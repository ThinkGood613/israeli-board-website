What counts as CE

  • Courses, workshops, conferences and webinars related to the ADC performance domains
  • Relevant academic courses at a recognized institution
  • Teaching or presenting on addiction topics, within a set limit per cycle

How to renew

  1. Complete your CE hours during the renewal cycle.
  2. Send your renewal form and CE certificates to ICBAP before your expiry date.
  3. Re-sign the Code of Ethics and disclose any relevant legal or disciplinary matters.
  4. Pay the renewal fee.

CE records & audit

Keep your certificates of attendance for at least one full cycle after you renew. ICBAP may ask to see them. Misrepresenting CE hours is an ethics violation.

Lapsed credentials

If you do not renew on time, your CASAP lapses and the registry shows it as "Expired". You may not use the CASAP title while it is lapsed. Contact ICBAP to learn how to reinstate it.
