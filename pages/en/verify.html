Verify a Professional

The public registry is the official source for checking whether someone holds a current CASAP credential.

Demo The registry currently shows fictional sample records. Connect it to ICBAP's credentialing database before launch.

What the registry shows

Each record shows the person's name, credential, any specialty endorsements, certificate number, city of practice, expiry date and current status.

Not listed?

Some people are certified under a previous name, and newly certified professionals appear within 5 business days. For an official written verification, contact us.

Public disciplinary actions

Final decisions to suspend or revoke a credential are published in line with our disciplinary policy.
